# Privacy Policy for PaxShare

**Last updated:** 30/01/2026

## 1. Introduction

PaxShare ("we", "us", "our") is an Android application designed to help UK private pilots create, sign, and store passenger cost sharing declaration forms in accordance with UK Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) requirements.

We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy in accordance with:

- UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)
- Data Protection Act 2018
- Google Play Developer Policies

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the PaxShare mobile application.
